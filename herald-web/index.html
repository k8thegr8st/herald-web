Herald

Internal tool of Hrana Stolu s.r.o. · Hrana Store, Úvoz 466/8, 602 00 Brno, Czech Republic · hrananetu.cz

Herald is an internal tool of Hrana Store, a trading card game shop in Brno. Once a week it publishes a short video with our store's upcoming tournament schedule and photos from last week's events to our own social media accounts (TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram). It is used only by our company for our own accounts; no other users or accounts are involved.
