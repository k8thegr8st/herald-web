Privacy Policy

Herald · Hrana Stolu s.r.o. · last updated 5 October 2026

Herald is an internal tool of Hrana Stolu s.r.o., company ID 19588941, Úvoz 466/8, 602 00 Brno, Czech Republic ("we", data controller). It automatically publishes our own content — the tournament schedule of our store and photos from our events — to our own social media accounts (TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram).

Data we process

When our company account on a social network is connected to Herald, we receive only the data necessary for publishing: the account identifier, display name and access tokens. We do not process data of any other users of the social networks, do not collect follower data and do not perform any tracking or profiling.

Purpose

The data is used solely to publish content to our own accounts. We do not share, sell or use it for advertising.

Storage, security and retention

Access tokens are stored encrypted in a secured storage (GitHub Secrets) accessible only to authorised staff, and kept only while we use the app. The connection can be revoked at any time in the settings of the social media account (on TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and websites); access then ends immediately and we delete the data.

Photos from our events

Videos published by Herald include photos from our events that we have already published on our social media, which may show event participants. We process them based on our legitimate interest in informing about and promoting our events (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) or on consent. We do not publish identifiable photos of children under 15 without parental consent. Any person may object at any time; if you do not want your photo to be published, contact us at info@hrananetu.cz and we will remove it and no longer use it.

Contact
